# StockEd — AI-Powered Financial Literacy & Investment Simulator

> An interactive financial education platform designed to help investment newcomers overcome the fear of financial loss, build investing literacy, and analyze market scenarios using cutting-edge AI.

---

## 🚀 Elevator Pitch
**StockEd** breaks down complex financial concepts and stock market data into easy-to-understand insights, allowing beginner investors to learn, simulate investment strategies, and make confident financial decisions powered by open AI models.

---

## ✨ Key Features
* **Interactive Financial Q&A:** Get instant, beginner-friendly explanations for complex market terms and trends.
* **Investment Risk Simulator:** Analyze hypothetical market scenarios without risking real money.
* **AI Market Analysis:** Leverages advanced AI models to synthesize key market metrics and trends.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Infrastructure
* **Language:** Python 3.10+
* **AI Infrastructure & APIs:** Nebius Token Factory API
* **AI Models:** Open-source NVIDIA AI models
* **Environment:** Python Virtual Environment (`venv`)

# StockEd — AI-Powered Financial Literacy & Investment Simulator

> An interactive financial education platform designed to help investment newcomers overcome the fear of financial loss, build investing literacy, and analyze market scenarios using cutting-edge AI.

---

## 🚀 Elevator Pitch
**StockEd** breaks down complex financial concepts and stock market data into easy-to-understand insights, allowing beginner investors to learn, simulate investment strategies, and make confident financial decisions powered by NVIDIA open-source AI models.

---

## ✨ Key Features
* **Interactive Financial Q&A:** Get instant, beginner-friendly explanations for complex market terms and trends powered by NVIDIA Nemotron.
* **Investment Risk Simulator:** Analyze hypothetical market scenarios without risking real money across stocks, real estate, and commodities.
* **AI Market Analysis:** Leverages advanced NVIDIA models to synthesize key market metrics and valuations.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Infrastructure
* **Language:** Python 3.10+
* **Framework:** Streamlit (UI & Dashboards)
* **AI Infrastructure & API:** NVIDIA NIM API (`integrate.api.nvidia.com`)[cite: 3]
* **AI Model:** NVIDIA Nemotron models (`nvidia/nemotron-3.5-lightning-30b-a3b`)[cite: 3]
* **Financial Data & Visuals:** `yfinance`, Plotly, SQLite, and Pandas
